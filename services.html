Common Questions

Service FAQs

Q. How long does it take to build a website?

A standard 5-page website usually takes 1-2 weeks. E-commerce or highly custom projects can take 4-8 weeks depending on complexity.

Q. Will my website be mobile-friendly?

Absolutely! 100% of the websites we build are fully responsive and optimized to look great on phones, tablets, and desktops.

Q. Do I have to write the content for the website?

You can provide the content, or we can write SEO-optimized content for you as an add-on service. We are flexible to your needs.

Q. Do you host the website?

Yes, we offer premium, fast, and secure hosting packages, complete with daily backups and SSL certificates.
